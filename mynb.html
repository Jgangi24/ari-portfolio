THE PROBLEM

Most loyalty programs reward one thing: spending money. That leaves out everyone who's genuinely passionate about a brand but not currently buying anything.

[Ari: replace with the real problem] New Balance wanted a way to reward the full range of how someone engages with the brand — training, following along on social, showing up to events — not just checkout activity. How might we design a rewards system broad enough to recognize all of that, while still feeling like a real achievement to climb through rather than a point-farming chore?

RESEARCH

Key insights that shaped the design

[Ari: swap in real research findings — user behavior, competitive loyalty programs] The strongest engagement signals weren't purchases at all — they were things like completing a workout via RunKeeper, checking in at a brand event, or following New Balance on social. The insight: build a single point economy that treats "training hard" and "buying gear" as equally legitimate ways to show passion for the brand, and give each one its own clear badge.

THE SOLUTION

Give every kind of brand engagement its own badge, and let people climb through tiers that actually feel earned.

01

Make the reward visible the moment someone joins

Downloading the app itself earns the first 500 points, shown immediately on a celebratory screen — establishing right away that engagement (not just spending) is what the program rewards. From there, the dashboard keeps current points, tier progress, and history all one tap away.

02

Make the tiers feel like a real climb, not just a number going up

[Ari: confirm/replace — describing tier thresholds and perks] Bronze, Silver, and Gold each get a distinct color identity across the entire dashboard — not just a label change — so leveling up feels like a visible shift in status, not a quiet backend update.

OUTCOME

A rewards system built around passion, not just purchases

[Ari: add real outcome — enrollment numbers, engagement lift, retention data] The finished system scales from a first-time download all the way to Gold-level status, with the same consistent dashboard and badge language carrying through every tier — giving New Balance a loyalty program that rewards the full relationship someone has with the brand, not just their receipts.
