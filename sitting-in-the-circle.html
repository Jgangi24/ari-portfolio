ILLUSTRATION & BOOK DESIGN

Sitting In The Circle

Sacred Observations from the Heart and other Internal Organs

A collection of short essays written by my father, drawn from his more than 40-year career as a psychologist. The stories are about love in the small places, off center stage — and I designed the book that holds them, cover to interior illustrations.

INTERIOR ILLUSTRATIONS
A weathered tree — resilience, shaped by time.
Height marks in a doorway — a small, quiet record of growing up.
A cracked shell and tracks leading forward — new beginnings.
A winding staircase, with small shoes left at the base.
